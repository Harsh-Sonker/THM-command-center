Ever lost a flag, misplaced an Nmap scan, or forgot your target IP in the middle of a CTF? 🤯 

Managing folders, terminal tabs, and text files during a TryHackMe or HackTheBox engagement can be an absolute nightmare. That's why I built the **THM Command Center** — a local-first, automated CLI environment for Kali Linux! 🛠️

It acts as your personal hacking OS. You define your target once (`thm start Overpass 10.10.10.10`), and the tool handles the rest:
📁 Instantly builds a perfect directory structure for your room.
🧠 Injects your target IP into every command automatically.
⏱️ Logs all command outputs, exit codes, and timings into a local SQLite database.
📓 Securely tracks your Flags, Credentials, Findings, and Notes.
🚀 Spawns a pre-configured 4-pane Tmux dashboard instantly (`thm tmux`).
📄 Generates a beautifully formatted Markdown executive report with 1 click (`thm report`).
